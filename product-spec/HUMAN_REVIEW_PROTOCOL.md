# Human review protocol — working draft

Status: required service component, not yet operational. The app does not currently collect reviewer identity or record a sign-off.

## Service boundary

- The ₹1,00,000 written report includes an editorial and method review by a named, qualified human before delivery.
- The separate ₹1,00,000 consultation is a distinct proposed service. It is not included in, implied by, or required to complete the report review; consultant, scope, and availability are not yet confirmed.
- Automated output remains a working draft. A price, PDF export, or completed calculation is not proof that a person reviewed it.
- Do not market the service as “accurate”, “guaranteed”, clairvoyant, medically/financially authoritative, or scientifically validated.

## Reviewer checklist

1. **Identity and scope:** Record reviewer name, relevant training/experience, review date, report version, and scope. Never imply a credential that was not verified.
2. **Calculation audit:** Independently recompute name-based values using the named letter-mapping system/version; recompute date-based values from their displayed formulas (the name-letter table does not change date cycles); check inputs, intermediate sums, reductions, and master-number rules.
3. **Claim provenance:** For each personal statement, identify the exact customer-supplied input or calculation that supports it. General symbolic interpretation must be labelled as tradition, not personal fact.
4. **Past events:** Confirm every dated event is quoted from the customer's own journal/context. Remove any claim that birth data “reveals” an undisclosed event. Do not present an entry as independently verified.
5. **Future content:** Keep scenarios conditional, time-bounded, falsifiable and reversible. Include evidence to watch, counter-signals and non-numerological practical constraints. Delete unsupported event predictions and invented probabilities.
6. **High-impact safety:** Remove or reframe deterministic claims about death, illness, trauma, pregnancy, marriage, crime, job outcomes, investment/wealth, or disasters. Never use fear to sell a remedy. Refer medical, legal, mental-health and financial decisions to qualified professionals.
7. **Remedies:** Check that each action is optional, low-cost, reversible, non-clinical, non-coercive, and does not promise protection or a result.
8. **Privacy and export:** Confirm consent scope; include only necessary user material; ensure the customer understands that print/PDF may contain sensitive notes and that current app storage is local to the device.
9. **Editorial quality:** Check the selected language, cultural wording, accessibility, internal consistency, missing context, and that the report plainly says when information is insufficient.
10. **Change log and sign-off:** Record which issues were corrected, which could not be resolved, and the reviewer’s final disposition. A reviewer may return a report for more information or decline sign-off; approval is never automatic.

## Minimum sign-off record before fulfillment exists

Capture: opaque report ID/version; calculation-method version; reviewer identity and verified scope; timestamp; pass/return/decline status; checklist result; required corrections; final customer-facing PDF hash/version; and whether the customer accepted the data handling and delivery terms. Store only the minimum personal data necessary, with access control, retention/deletion rules, and an audit trail. No such record or secure fulfillment flow exists in the current local-only app.

## Still needs owner approval

- Reviewer(s), credentials/training standards, conflict-of-interest policy, and who can authorize release.
- Turnaround, included revisions, support period, report format/languages, refund/cancellation terms, and delivery method.
- Privacy notice, data retention/deletion rules, access controls, consent wording, and applicable legal review.
- Whether any Vedic astrology input is in scope; the current flagship report stays numerology-first and does not silently blend systems.
